# IBERFIT exercise visual standard v1

- Canonical visual package per exercise: required `movement` asset plus optional `thumbnail`, `start`, `end` and `demo` assets under the exact canonical exercise-ID namespace.
- `start` and `end` should show the same approved athlete, outfit, gym, camera language and equipment setup; only the movement phase changes.
- A `movement` asset may combine start/end when useful, but must remain a clean instructional visual rather than a poster or text-heavy infographic.
- The exercise is always the visual protagonist: frame tightly enough that the movement is easy to read at card size, while keeping all biomechanically relevant joints, load and supports visible.
- Backgrounds use the approved premium dark-gym language but stay deliberately subdued, defocused and lower-contrast than the athlete. Gym architecture, props and decorative lighting must never compete with the movement.
- No `posición inicial`/`posición final` labels, slogans, explanatory copy, exercise titles or decorative text inside the image.
- Same approved male athlete and premium dark gym language across the library.
- Black technical outfit; the **only IBERFIT branding permitted inside the generated visual is the exact official isotipo, small on the shirt**. No wordmark, no wall logo, no equipment logo, no shorts logo, no repeated isotipo and no invented marks.
- Use the official repository asset for the shirt isotipo. Never redraw or approximate it.
- Optional compact anatomical inset only when it improves comprehension and does not obscure biomechanics. Keep it visually secondary and consistent with the approved library.
- Before setting `human_approved=true`, compare the candidate against at least one currently approved library asset for crop, background prominence, branding restraint and anatomical-inset scale.
- Biomechanics must be instructionally correct; generated assets remain quarantined until both biomechanics and visual QA are approved.
- Publication is idempotent, exact-ID based and fail closed.
